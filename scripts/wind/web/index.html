Profile

Weight

Mass = bounding-box volume. Lighter things get × (ref / mass)^exponent (capped), and on the ground the wind must beat grip × mass before anything moves.

Curves

log: scale × ln(1 + d / ref), rising forever. approach: closes half the gap from start to max every half-life blocks, never reaching it.
