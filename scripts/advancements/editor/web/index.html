    Notes

    • Ids never change. Advancements move tabs by parent only, so nobody loses progress.
    • Copies: a tab can be headed by a copy of an advancement in another tab. Earning either earns both, and that unlocks the tab. "Open a copy as a new tab" makes a new pair.
    • Band journey: the reached-a-band advancements chain in band order when the game loads. Moving the first one moves the whole chain; the others follow the band order.
    • Required values are the ones shipped in the mod. A relay override can still change them on a live server.
    • Every advancement is drawn, hidden ones included. In game, hidden ones stay hidden until earned.
