## 2. UI lines — 115, grouped by feature

### 2a. Shared / drifting carriages — 49 lines
*(PRs #860, #862, #863, #874, #875, #876)*

The biggest new feature since your pass: carriages that players build get shared to a relay
and travel into other players' worlds.

| Group | Keys | What it is |
|---|---:|---|
| `chat.dungeontrain.shared_carriage.*` | 33 | flavour lines shown when you board a shared carriage, plus 4 noun variants used inside them |
| `advancements.dungeontrain.dungeon_train.drift_*` | 12 | four new advancements (titles, descriptions, hints) |
| `command.dungeontrain.report_carriage.*` | 4 | `/report_carriage` moderation command responses |

**⚠️ Terminology decision needed.** This feature introduces vocabulary the mod has never had
before, and the machine picked the Chinese without you. Please settle these — the whole group
depends on them being consistent:

| English | Current machine Chinese | Where |
|---|---|---|
| drifting carriage | 漂流车厢 | the primary term, used everywhere |
| drifting cart | 漂流小车 | noun variant 2 |
| carriage adrift | 随波车厢 | noun variant 3 |
| drifting room | 漂流空间 | noun variant 4 |
| **A Drift Gift** (advancement) | 漂流的礼物 | also appears as *drift-gift* in a book, §3 |

Note the 33 flavour lines use `%s` for the noun, so the four noun variants get substituted
into every one of them — they must all read naturally in that slot.

### 2b. Adult / Kid mode + the consent screen — 20 lines
*(PR #868)*

A new first-run screen asking who is playing, which gates player-written content and developer
chat.

- `gui.dungeontrain.consent.*` (17) — the consent/permissions screen: player-written content,
  telemetry, other players' mob reincarnations, developer chat.
- `gui.dungeontrain.content_mode.*` (3) — "Who is playing?" / Adult / Kid.

**⚠️ Tone check.** Two of these lines are deliberate jokes — `consent.harvest_soul` ("Harvest
your soul") and its tips ("I looked into it. The paperwork was appalling." / "Kids' souls are
the good ones. You get safer books. I get your soul. Fair trade."). They should read as
obviously comic in Chinese, not as a real data-collection request sitting next to the genuine
telemetry toggle. Worth your judgement more than most lines here.

### 2c. Options + Editor Settings screens — 21 lines
*(PR #870)*

- `gui.dungeontrain.options.*` (14) — the Dungeon Train options screen: internet master switch,
  content mode, snapshot chat log, snapshot max resolution, AUTO.
- `gui.dungeontrain.editor_settings.*` (7) — display-scale settings: All Displays, Worldspace,
  HUD.

Mostly short labels + tooltips. `AUTO` may be fine left as-is.

### 2d. Political Content Filter — 7 lines
*(PR #869)*

`gui.dungeontrain.political_filter.*` — a filter, shown to Chinese-language clients, that hides
community books and player letters flagged as politically sensitive.

**⚠️ This is the most sensitive copy in the batch.** It is player-facing text about political
content, written in English by a non-Chinese developer and machine-translated. Please rewrite
it however it needs to read for a Chinese player rather than tracking the English closely — and
tell me if the framing itself is wrong.

### 2e. China payment copy — 6 lines
*(PRs #857, #867, #880)*

- `gui.dungeontrain.support.*` (4) and `gui.dungeontrain.death.donate_cn*` (2).
- This is an **entirely new payment route** — none of it existed when you last reviewed. It is
  **WeChat Pay only**. (Alipay was briefly added and then removed again within this same
  window, so it never reached a build you'd have seen; there is no 支付宝 copy to review.)
- One key, `gui.dungeontrain.support.financial.desc.cn.modpack`, is the **modpack variant** of
  the support blurb and has **no English source** — it exists only in Chinese. Treat the
  existing Chinese as the original and edit it directly (it should say 整合包, not 模组).

### 2f. Book-vote familiarity lines — 9 lines
*(older feature, translated in the #870 backlog sweep)*

`chat.dungeontrain.familiar_book.*` — messages shown when a book you wrote comes back to you,
with its up/down vote counts. These use positional placeholders (`%1$s`, `%2$s`, `%3$s`) and
have singular/plural forms — the Chinese doesn't need the plural distinction, but both forms
must still be filled in.

### 2g. Cheat-mod detection — 3 lines
*(PR #843, translated in the #870 backlog sweep)*

`chat.dungeontrain.free_play.cheat_mod*` — shown when a known cheat mod is detected and the run
drops to Free Play.
